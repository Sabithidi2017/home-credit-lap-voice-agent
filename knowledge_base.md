=====================================================================
MOHAMED'S VOICE AGENT — LOAN AGAINST PROPERTY (LAP)
PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE
=====================================================================

This document contains the ONLY product information the AI Voice
Agent is allowed to quote during preliminary qualification calls.
Anything NOT present in this document must be deferred to the
senior loan expert.

=====================================================================
1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
=====================================================================

Product Name:
  Loan Against Property (LAP)

Issuer / Brand:
  Mohamed's Voice Agent (in partnership with Home Credit)

Purpose:
  A secured loan offered to existing, valued customers against
  the mortgage of a residential, commercial, or industrial
  property they own.

Target Customer:
  Existing customers who own a property (sole or joint) and
  meet the eligibility criteria.

Nature of Offer:
  Pre-approved, loyalty-reward offer. This call is a PRELIMINARY
  QUALIFICATION, not a final approval.

=====================================================================
2. LOAN AMOUNT
=====================================================================

Minimum Loan Amount:
  ₹5,00,000 (5 Lakhs)

Maximum Loan Amount:
  ₹75,00,000 (75 Lakhs)

If the customer requests more than ₹75 Lakhs:
  - Do NOT disqualify.
  - Explain the maximum limit.
  - Offer to proceed with ₹75 Lakhs.
  - If customer declines → politely close the call.

=====================================================================
3. TENURE
=====================================================================

Minimum Tenure:
  3 years

Maximum Tenure:
  15 years

Any tenure below 3 years or above 15 years is ineligible.

=====================================================================
4. ELIGIBLE PROPERTY TYPES
=====================================================================

Eligible:
  - Residential (house, flat, apartment, villa)
  - Commercial (shop, office, showroom, warehouse)
  - Industrial (factory, manufacturing unit)

Not Eligible:
  - Agricultural land / farm land / kheti ki zameen

=====================================================================
5. OWNERSHIP
=====================================================================

Eligible:
  - Sole ownership
  - Joint ownership (with spouse, family member, or business partner)

Both are treated equally.

=====================================================================
6. DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS
=====================================================================

Mandatory:
  - Original property documents must be available for verification.

Not Accepted:
  - Photocopies only
  - Lost documents
  - Documents held by a third party who will not release them

Acceptable Even If Not Physically With Customer:
  - "Documents are at home"
  - "Documents are with my CA / lawyer"
  - "Documents are in a bank locker" (as long as producible)

=====================================================================
7. OCCUPATION & INCOME
=====================================================================

Eligible Occupations:
  - Salaried
  - Self-employed

Eligible Income Modes:
  - Income received via Bank (direct deposit, NEFT, IMPS, UPI to bank)

Not Eligible:
  - Income received primarily in Cash

Combination Matrix:
  Salaried + Bank          → Eligible
  Self-employed + Bank     → Eligible
  Salaried + Cash          → Not Eligible
  Self-employed + Cash     → Not Eligible

=====================================================================
8. MARKET VALUE OF PROPERTY
=====================================================================

The estimated current market value of the property is captured
during the call.

There is NO minimum or maximum market value threshold for this
preliminary offer. The senior loan expert will assess valuation
during underwriting.

=====================================================================
9. INTEREST RATE
=====================================================================

Exact interest rate is NOT disclosed during the preliminary call.

Interest rate depends on:
  - Property valuation
  - Customer's credit profile
  - Tenure selected
  - Loan amount
  - Current market rates

If the customer asks: "The exact interest rate will be shared by
our senior loan expert after the preliminary qualification."

=====================================================================
10. PROCESSING FEE
=====================================================================

Processing fee details are NOT disclosed during the preliminary
call. The senior loan expert will share all applicable charges.

=====================================================================
11. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED AT APPLICATION STAGE
=====================================================================

(For informational purposes — do NOT demand these during the
preliminary call. Only confirm original property documents are
available.)

Typical documents requested later by the senior loan expert:
  - Original property documents (title deed, sale deed)
  - PAN card
  - Aadhaar card
  - Latest 3 months' bank statements
  - Latest 3 months' salary slips (if salaried)
  - Latest 2 years' ITR (if self-employed)
  - Property tax receipts
  - No Objection Certificate (NOC) from society/builder (if
    applicable)

=====================================================================
12. TRANSFER / BALANCE TRANSFER CASES
=====================================================================

If the customer already has an existing loan on the property,
OR wants to reduce their current EMI,
OR wants to transfer their existing loan:

  → This is NOT handled by the preliminary AI agent.
  → Inform the customer a loan-transfer specialist will contact
    them shortly.
  → End the call.

=====================================================================
13. CALL HANDOFF
=====================================================================

After all 7 eligibility points are captured and criteria are
met, the customer is handed off to a SENIOR LOAN EXPERT.

The senior expert will:
  - Explain exact interest rate
  - Explain processing fee and other charges
  - Guide the customer through documentation
  - Complete formal application and underwriting

NEVER say "you are approved" or "your loan is sanctioned."
The AI agent only QUALIFIES leads; the senior expert decides
final approval.

=====================================================================
14. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (Quick Reference)
=====================================================================

Q. What is the maximum loan I can get?
A. Up to ₹75 Lakhs under this pre-approved offer.

Q. What is the minimum tenure?
A. 3 years. Maximum is 15 years.

Q. Can I take the loan on a jointly owned property?
A. Yes, joint ownership is eligible.

Q. I have agricultural land. Can I mortgage it?
A. No, agricultural land is not eligible for this offer.

Q. I receive my income in cash. Am I eligible?
A. No, income must be received into a bank account.

Q. I have an existing loan on this property.
A. This would be handled by our loan-transfer specialist, who
   will contact you shortly.

Q. What documents do I need right now?
A. For this preliminary call, I only need to confirm that your
   original property documents are available for verification.
   The senior loan expert will guide you on full documentation.

Q. What interest rate will I get?
A. The exact interest rate will be provided by our senior loan
   expert after the preliminary qualification.

Q. Are there any processing charges?
A. All applicable charges will be explained by the senior loan
   expert. I am unable to confirm fees on this call.

Q. How soon will the senior expert call me?
A. Shortly after this call. You will receive a call-back from
   our senior loan expert.

Q. Where is your nearest branch?
A. This will be shared by the senior loan expert during the
   follow-up call.

Q. Is this a guaranteed loan?
A. This is a preliminary qualification. Final approval is subject
   to the senior loan expert's assessment and verification.

=====================================================================
15. COMPLIANCE & DISCLOSURE NOTES
=====================================================================

The AI agent must:
  - Never promise approval
  - Never quote interest rates not in this document
  - Never quote processing fees not in this document
  - Never invent eligibility rules
  - Always identify itself as an AI Voice Assistant when asked
  - Always offer to end the call if the customer is unavailable
  - Never continue after a disqualifying answer

END OF KNOWLEDGE BASE
